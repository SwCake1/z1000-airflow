# Third-party materials

## Three.js

The browser viewers use Three.js 0.186.1, licensed under MIT.
The build includes the unmodified license at
`simulation/node_modules/three/LICENSE`.

Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js

## MakeHuman

The rider meshes derive from MakeHuman core assets (body/clothing helpers,
weights and macro targets) distributed under CC0. Clothing, motorcycle helmets
and riding poses were adapted for this project.

The supplied asset license is preserved in
[LICENSE.ASSETS.md](simulation/assets/makehuman/LICENSE.ASSETS.md).

Source: https://www.makehumancommunity.org/about/license.html

## Motorcycle and calculations

The motorcycle geometry and textures were reconstructed from the owner's
photographs. The source photographs are not included in this repository.
Kawasaki and Givi names identify the depicted motorcycle and accessory;
this project is not affiliated with either manufacturer.

The flow fields were calculated locally with OpenFOAM Foundation 14.
OpenFOAM executables are not distributed with the website.
